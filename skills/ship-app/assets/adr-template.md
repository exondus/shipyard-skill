# NNNN — <the decision, as a statement not a question>

Date: <date>
Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN

## Context

What is true that forces a choice. Constraints, deadlines, what was already decided, what was
measured. Not the options yet — just the situation. If someone reads only this section they should be
able to guess the shape of the decision.

## Options considered

| Option | For | Against |
|---|---|---|

Include the option that was obviously wrong, and say why it was obviously wrong. A year from now that
is the part nobody remembers, and it is what stops the decision being reopened every quarter.

## Decision

One paragraph, in the active voice. What was chosen and the single most important reason.

## Consequences

What this makes easy. What it makes hard. What now cannot be changed cheaply, and what the exit would
cost if it has to be.

## What would make us revisit this

A condition, not a date. "If our monthly bill on this passes X." "If the library goes six months
without a release." "If we need a second write region." Naming the trigger is what makes a decision
reviewable instead of permanent.
